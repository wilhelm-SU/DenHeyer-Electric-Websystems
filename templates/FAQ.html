Frequently Asked Questions

General Services

What types of electrical work do you do?

We provide residential, commercial, and industrial electrical services, including new installations, renovations, repairs, troubleshooting, generator installations, EV charger installations, pool and hot tub wiring, smoke detector installation, and more.

Do you handle both small repairs and large projects?

Yes. We work on everything from replacing outlets and light fixtures to complete electrical renovations and commercial installations.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are fully licensed and insured.

Do you offer free estimates?

We provide estimates for most installation and renovation projects. Contact us to discuss your project.

What areas do you serve?

Sussex County, NJ, Pike County, PA, and surrounding areas.

Residential Electrical

Can you help with electrical issues found during a home inspection?

Yes. We repair code violations, outdated wiring, faulty outlets, missing GFCI protection, panel issues, and other electrical problems commonly identified during home inspections.

Can you upgrade my electrical panel?

Yes. We install and upgrade electrical panels to improve safety and accommodate increased electrical demands.

Do you install smoke and carbon monoxide detectors?

Yes. We install, replace, and repair smoke and carbon monoxide detectors to help keep your home protected and code compliant.

Can you install new lighting and ceiling fans?

Yes. We install indoor and outdoor lighting, recessed lighting, ceiling fans, under-cabinet lighting, and more.

Generator Services

Do you install standby generators?

Yes. We install whole-home standby generators that automatically provide backup power during outages.

Do you install portable generator connections?

Yes. We install interlock kits and generator inlets so portable generators can be connected safely.

Do you service and troubleshoot generators?

Yes. We diagnose, repair, and maintain whole-house generators.

How often should my generator be serviced?

Most manufacturers recommend annual maintenance or after a specified number of operating hours.

EV Charger Installation

Can you install an EV charger at my home?

Yes. We install Level 2 charging stations for most electric vehicle brands.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. We'll evaluate your electrical service to determine whether a panel upgrade is necessary.

Can you install commercial EV charging stations?

Yes. We install charging stations for businesses, workplaces, and commercial properties.

Pools & Hot Tubs

Do you wire pools and hot tubs?

Yes. We provide complete electrical installations for pools, spas, and hot tubs, including dedicated circuits, disconnects, bonding, and GFCI protection.

Can you upgrade electrical service for a new hot tub?

Yes. We can install the required circuits and make any necessary electrical upgrades.

Commercial & Industrial

Do you perform commercial electrical renovations?

Yes. We complete renovations, tenant fit-outs, lighting upgrades, equipment installations, and electrical system improvements.

Can you troubleshoot electrical problems in commercial buildings?

Yes. We diagnose electrical issues quickly to minimize downtime.

Do you work with industrial facilities?

Yes. We perform industrial electrical installations, repairs, maintenance, and equipment wiring.

Safety & Troubleshooting

Why do my breakers keep tripping?

Frequent breaker trips can indicate overloaded circuits, faulty appliances, damaged wiring, or electrical faults. We can diagnose the cause and recommend the appropriate repair.

When should I replace my electrical panel?

If your panel is outdated, frequently trips breakers, feels hot, shows signs of corrosion, or cannot handle your home's electrical needs, it may be time for an upgrade.

Do you troubleshoot electrical problems?

Yes. We diagnose flickering lights, dead outlets, breaker issues, power loss, faulty switches, and other electrical problems.

Scheduling

How quickly can you schedule service?

Scheduling depends on the type of project and our current workload. Contact us and we'll schedule your service as soon as possible.

What should I do if I lose power?

If only your property has lost power, check your breakers first. If the issue persists, contact us. If the entire neighborhood is without power, contact your utility company.

How can I request a quote?

Call us or fill out our contact form with details about your project, and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
