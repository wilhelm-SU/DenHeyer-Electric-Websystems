Our History

Our father was an electrician for many years, working with different companies. He and his best friend decided to take a leap of faith and start their own electric company. Sadly, shortly after they started the company, our father passed away unexpectedly.

For as long as I can remember, my brother has always talked about living out many of our father’s dreams. One of those dreams was becoming an electrician and starting his own company.

Wayne began working as a fabricator at a trailer company, where he built trailers, usually handling the wiring, working alongside his two cousins and best friend. Eventually, he became an electrician, working with some of the same companies our father once did.

In 2015, Wayne started his own company. He began with just a truck and a few hand tools, building a reputation solely through word of mouth. One day, he asked me to help him create a logo that would honor our father. After many versions, we chose the light bulb guy with Coke-bottle glasses, a big mustache, and a smile from ear to ear—a reflection of the smile our dad always wore. That little character is a constant reminder of him.

Over the years, our company has grown in so many ways. As the workload increased, friends were always there to lend a hand. Today, we’re proud to be a team of eight.
